More About Me

I design and build things people actually enjoy using.

I'm Steven — most people know me as tevo. I'm a multi-disciplinary designer and developer based in Egypt, working across UI/UX design, web design, front-end development and AI-assisted creative production.

Over the last four years I've moved between agency work, freelance client projects and my own ventures — which means I think about design the way a founder does: not just "does it look good," but "does it work, and does it move the needle."

When I'm not designing interfaces, I'm usually deep in a video edit, sketching a brand identity, or tinkering with AI-driven workflows to speed up the creative process without losing the craft.

Let's Talk
What I Believe

Good design is invisible until it's missing. I believe the best digital experiences don't announce themselves — they just make sense, from the first click to the last. Every project starts with a question: what is this person actually trying to do, and what's in their way?

I also believe craft and speed aren't opposites. AI-assisted workflows let me move faster on production work — reels, iterations, asset variations — so more time goes into the decisions that actually need a human: the strategy, the hierarchy, the feel.

My Approach

I treat every brief as a source of truth, not a suggestion. Whether I'm implementing a client's high-fidelity design or starting from a blank canvas, the goal is the same: preserve intent, sweat the details, and never ship something that feels templated.

I work in tight, visible loops — rough structure first, then real content, then polish — so whoever I'm working with always has something concrete to react to instead of waiting on a big reveal.

My Process
Step 01

Discover

Understand the goal, the audience and the constraints before opening any design tool.

Step 02

Structure

Map the flow — wireframes, sitemaps, or a shot list, depending on the medium.

Step 03

Design

Bring it to life with a visual system that's distinct, not default.

Step 04

Build & Refine

Develop, test, and tighten every interaction and edge case.

Step 05

Deliver

Ship, hand off cleanly, and stay around for the follow-up questions.
